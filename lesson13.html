Math · MA.6.NSO.3.5 I Do

Same Value, Three Different Looks

The Comets made 3 out of 4 free throws in the championship game — one value, written three ways:

Fraction: 3/4. Decimal: 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75. Percent: 0.75 × 100 = 75%.

Test Tactic

Fraction to decimal: divide top by bottom. Decimal to percent: move the decimal point two places right (or multiply by 100). The value never changes — only how it's written.

Math · MA.6.GR.2.3 I Do

Filling the Trophy Case

The tournament trophy case is a rectangular prism: 3 ft long, 2 ft wide, 4 ft tall.

Volume = length × width × height = 3 × 2 × 4 = 24 cubic feet.

Test Tactic

Volume always uses THREE dimensions multiplied together — not just two, like area does. Watch for the extra measurement.

ELA · ELA.6.R.3.1 I Do

Two Kinds of Figurative Language

Simile: compares two things using "like," "as," or "the way." "Her focus was as steady as a held note."

Personification: gives a non-human thing a human action or quality. "The scoreboard seemed to hold its breath."

Watch for both as you read the final scene.

Read · Narrative

Two Shots

The gym went quiet the way a held breath goes quiet. Jasmine stood at the free-throw line with the whole tournament riding on two shots, and for a second the scoreboard lights felt like the only thing still moving in the building.

She'd missed shots like this before — plenty of them, actually — but tonight her hands felt steady in a way they hadn't back in Week 3. The ball left her fingers and the whole gym seemed to lean forward with it.

Two swishes later, the Comets were Winter Classic champions. Coach Whitfield didn't call a timeout. He didn't need to call anything. The scoreboard said everything that mattered.
