Math Recap

Two Quick Reminders

Signed numbers: a gain is positive, a loss is negative. Absolute value is the distance from zero, direction doesn't matter.

Ratios and percentages: simplify a ratio by dividing both sides by their GCF. To turn a ratio into a percent, divide the first number by the second, then multiply by 100.

That's it — straight into the blitz.

ELA Recap

The Scene, in Three Lines

Marcus drops a pass, then gets tackled four yards behind the line on the very next play. Coach Whitfield sits with him — not to blame him, but to point out that Marcus sprinted back to make the tackle instead of sulking. Coach's plan: run the same route in practice until it's automatic. That's the remedy for a drop — more reps, not more worrying.
